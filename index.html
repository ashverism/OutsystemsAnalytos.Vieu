Your Market Moment

Here is what we found.

Manufacturers don't need more dashboards. They need to see a shortage coming before it stops the line. Supplier performance, lead times and demand keep shifting, but the buffer settings in most ERPs stay fixed until someone changes them by hand. Leaders also want AI that recommends while a planner still decides. Stockly is built for that gap: it watches every SKU and supplier, predicts risk, recalibrates buffers, and sends every recommendation through a human approval gate.

Analytos · The solution builder
Production-ready agents for manufacturing
Analytos Labs is led by Sunil Ranka and focuses on manufacturing solutions. Stockly is one of several it is releasing. It is an OutSystems solution partner, building "agentic systems on the OutSystems platform." Its pitch is built in 2 weeks, live in 90 days. The team includes Narayan Laksham, a manufacturing and supply-chain leader who drives go-to-market and partnerships.4
Stockly · The offer

Inventory intelligence on top of the ERP the customer already runs (SAP, Oracle, QAD, Epicor, Infor, NetSuite), with no rip-and-replace. Stockly runs natively on OutSystems, is installed per plant, and is aimed at the 500–2,000 SKU book where supplier slips go unnoticed and planning lags. It learns each supplier's real delivery behaviour for each part from historical receipts. The result is a lead-time distribution rather than a single planning number, and Stockly feeds it back into Kanban card sizes, reorder points and safety stock. It keeps a Supplier Reliability Index and has a Plan for Every Part built in, not kept in a separate spreadsheet. Every recommendation passes a human approval gate.5
