Your Market Moment

Here is what we found.

Manufacturers don't need more dashboards. They need to see a shortage coming before it stops the line. Supplier performance, lead times and demand keep shifting, but the buffer settings in most ERPs stay fixed until someone changes them by hand. Leaders also want AI that recommends while a planner still decides. Stockly is built for that gap: it watches every SKU and supplier, predicts risk, recalibrates buffers, and sends every recommendation through a human approval gate.

The GTM Challenge

The product story is sharp and the entry offer is low-risk. The harder part is reach and ownership. The people who feel the pain sit in supply chain and operations, not IT. And with three companies in one motion, a warm path can easily land with nobody. These are our pre-kickoff hypotheses. We will test them with you on the call.

A 500–2,000 SKU book where planners still tune buffers and min/max by handBuyers sit in supply chain and operations. A platform vendor's usual IT relationships may not reach them
Volatile suppliers and lead times. The ERP shows what happened, not what's comingThree parties in one motion. Without named owners, leads fall between OutSystems and Analytos
A concrete, low-risk first step: a live agent on one plant's real data in 14 daysA crowded field of planning suites and AI vendors chasing the same budget
A human approval gate on every action, which matches how leaders want to adopt AIFatigue with AI pilots that never reach production
